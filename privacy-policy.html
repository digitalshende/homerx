Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15/01/2024

Welcome to HomeRx. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our patients' personal information. This privacy policy explains the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the safeguards we have in place to protect it.

Information Collection

  • Personal Identification Information: We collect names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and other contact details to provide our services.
  • Health Information: Medical histories, symptoms, treatment information, and other health-related data are collected solely for the purpose of providing personalized healthcare services.
  • Payment Information: We collect necessary payment information, which may include credit card numbers or insurance details, to process payments for our services.

Use of Information

  • Service Delivery: Personal and health information is used to schedule visits, provide medical care, and manage ongoing treatment plans.
  • Billing: Payment information is used to complete transactions and process insurance claims.
  • Communication: Contact details are used to send appointment confirmations, health tips, service updates, and promotional offers.
  • Improvement of Services: We analyze information to improve our healthcare services and patient experience.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may share information with trusted partners who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing patients, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose patient information when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

Data Security

  • Safeguards: We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of personal information, including encryption, firewalls, and access controls.
  • Data Retention: Personal information is retained only as long as necessary for the fulfillment of the services provided and as required by law.

Patient Rights

  • Access and Control: Patients have the right to access their personal information, correct inaccuracies, and request the deletion of their data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Consent: By using our services, patients consent to our collection and use of personal information as outlined in this policy.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

Updates: We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify patients of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
