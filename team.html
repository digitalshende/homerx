Trust & Safety First

Why Trust the HomeRx?

We understand that inviting healthcare professionals into your home requires complete trust. That is why HomeRx enforces a strict onboarding protocol for every practitioner joining our team:

Registered with Medical Council

Every general practitioner holds valid registrations with the Medical Council of India (MCI) / State Medical Council with verified clinical expertise.

Rigorous Background Checks

All doctors, technicians, and nursing staff undergo thorough background checks prior to onboarding.

Strict Safety & Hygiene

Our team follows rigorous infection control guidelines, using sanitized equipment, fresh gloves, and masks on every visit.

Health Record Management

All consultations, prescriptions, and radiology reports are securely documented and managed by the HomeRx Operations Department.
