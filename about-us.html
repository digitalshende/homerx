Our Mission & Core Values

Our Mission

To revolutionize home healthcare across Maharashtra by making Certified General Physician & General Practitioner consultation consultations, portable diagnostic imaging, and essential medical procedures fast, safe, transparent, and affordable for every family.

Why Families Trust HomeRx:

  • Patient-Centered Compassion We treat every patient with the care, empathy, and personal attention they deserve.
  • Clinical Excellence All treatments and diagnostic procedures strictly adhere to established medical protocols.
  • Speed & Punctuality Our dedicated care coordinators ensure prompt dispatch within 30 to 90 minutes across all active service regions.
  • Hygiene & Safety First Strict infection control protocols, sanitized medical equipment, fresh gloves, and masks for every visit.
  • Transparent & Fair Pricing Clear, upfront consultation and service fees with no hidden costs.

What We Offer – Our Integrated At-Home Healthcare Ecosystem

To ensure comprehensive medical management without hospital stress, HomeRx offers a wide range of integrated home healthcare services:

Service Category Services Included
Doctor At Door Home visits by certified General Physicians for General & acute illnesses, Chronic disease management (like Hypertension, Diabetes, Thyroid etc.)
Portable Home Diagnostics Doorstep portable digital X-rays (orthopaedic), digital ECG testing, and blood sample collection with same-day digital reports.
Medical Procedures at Home Professional sterile wound dressing, IV drip setup, antibiotic administration, Foley’s catheterization, and Ryle’s tube (NG tube) insertion.
Elderly & Bedridden Care Regular family physician reviews, post-hospitalization checkups, and routine monitoring for mobility-restricted senior citizens.

Qualified Medical Professionals & Rigorous Onboarding

Your safety and trust are our highest priorities. Every medical professional associated with HomeRx undergoes strict credential verification before onboarding:

  • Medical Council Registered: Every general practitioner on our team holds valid registrations with the Medical Council and proven clinical experience.
