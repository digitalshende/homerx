1. Care Call (Single Visit On-Demand Plan)

The Care Call plan is designed for individuals and families who require immediate, single-session medical attention without long-term subscription commitments. Starting at just ₹499/- per session, this plan provides a fully equipped, Medical Council-registered General Physician visit directly to your home or office..

During the visit, our verified doctor performs an on-site physical examination, diagnoses your symptoms, prescribes necessary medications, and logs your digital health report securely with the HomeRx Operations Department. This pay-per-visit option covers one person for one session and is ideal for managing sudden seasonal fevers, acute viral flu, food poisoning, stomach pain, or routine health evaluations. Please note that exact visit charges may vary slightly depending on your specific city and location.

2. Basic Plan (3-Month Routine Care Package)

The Basic Plan offers structured primary healthcare support for individuals who need regular medical checkups over a short term. Priced at ₹2,999/- (plus a one-time ₹499/- registration fee), this subscription remains valid for 3 full months and covers one person..

The package includes up to 12 Doctor@Door home visits, allowing for routine monthly or weekly physician consultations right in the comfort of your home. It is tailored specifically for senior citizens, bedridden patients, or individuals managing ongoing health conditions like high blood pressure (hypertension) and diabetes who require continuous medical supervision without the physical strain of traveling to crowded clinics. Charges may vary depending on your specific location and city.

3. D@D Plan (6-Month Dual Care Package)

Our D@D Plan is HomeRx's most popular healthcare package, providing flexible, medium-term medical coverage for up to two individuals. Available at ₹4,999/- (plus a one-time ₹499/- registration fee), this subscription is valid for 6 months and covers two persons.

Subscribers receive up to 24 Doctor@Door visits to their residence or workplace, making it an excellent choice for elderly couples, post-operative recovery monitoring, or chronic disease management for two family members. The D@D plan ensures continuous medical oversight, regular health assessments, and prompt clinical care whenever seasonal or chronic health issues arise. Location-based price variations may apply.

4. Plus + Plan (1-Year Annual Family Package)

The Plus + Plan is our complete primary healthcare subscription designed to cover your entire household under one comprehensive plan. Priced at ₹9,999/- (plus a one-time ₹499/- registration fee) with a validity of 1 full year, this plan covers your whole family..

The Plus + Plan includes up to 52 Doctor@Door home visits, effectively providing your family with the convenience of a dedicated family physician visiting your home every week of the year. From treating seasonal childhood infections and managing elderly parents' chronic conditions to post-surgery follow-ups, this annual package delivers complete primary healthcare, fast dispatch, and digital health record management with zero clinic commute stress.
